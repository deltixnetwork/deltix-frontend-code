Deltix Chain Live

Token supply

Monetary model

Ethereum-inspired issuance & burn
St+1 = St + It − Bt — no fixed max supply. Protocol issuance funds validator rewards while transaction base-fee burning can offset inflation. Staking locks tokens and reduces liquid circulating supply (C = S − L − R).

Participation roles

Staker / Delegator You
Lock $DLTX and delegate to qualified validators from your phone. Your share: Ru = (Su/Sv) · Rv · (1 − cv).
Validator
Runs consensus nodes, proposes blocks and attests. Bonded stake is subject to inactivity penalties and slashing for consensus violations.
Your phone is a gateway — not a miner
Deltix does not simulate mining on your phone. Smartphones control wallets, staking, delegation, governance and D-Browser access; validator nodes run on dedicated infrastructure.

Referral program Max 3

Each verified account may sponsor a maximum of three participants. No downlines, no paid membership, no rewards for registration alone. Rewards activate only after the referred participant verifies and maintains an eligible stake. Manage yours in the Community tab.

Governance

Deltix DAO Live
The DAO is live from day one: protocol changes are decided by stake-weighted community vote in the Community tab — 1 staked $DLTX = 1 vote. The scope of binding on-chain control (validator voting, timelocks, treasury) expands progressively toward full decentralization.

Liquidity strategy

Deltix — Proof of Stake, Ethereum-inspired programmable utility, delivered as a standalone mobile-first application.
