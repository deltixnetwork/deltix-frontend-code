Delete Your Account

Deltix Network ($DLTX) · Developer: Deltix Network · Applies to the Deltix Network app

You can permanently delete your Deltix Network account and its associated data at any time. Deletion is available directly inside the app and takes effect immediately — no email, phone call, or support request is required.

1. How to Delete Your Account In-App

  1. Open the Deltix Network app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Community tab.
  3. Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete forever.

⚠ Deletion is immediate and irreversible. Your wallet, any $DLTX balance, active stakes, rewards, and referral history are permanently destroyed and cannot be recovered. $DLTX has no monetary value, is not redeemable for real currency, and no refund or compensation applies to deleted balances.

2. Alternative: Request Deletion by Email

If you can no longer access the app, email support@deltixllc.com from the email address registered to your account with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We verify ownership of the address and complete the deletion within 30 days.

3. What Is Deleted

DataAction on deletion
Account (email, verification status)Permanently deleted immediately
Wallet (address, keys, balances)Permanently deleted immediately; active stakes are released and destroyed
Stakes, rewards, transaction historyPermanently deleted immediately
Referral links (sponsor / referrals)Unlinked immediately; your referrals' accounts are unaffected
DAO votesYour vote records are deleted; already-finalized proposal outcomes are not recalculated
One-time passcodesPermanently deleted immediately

4. Data That May Be Retained

  • Server and security logs (IP address, request metadata) — retained up to 90 days for fraud prevention and security, then deleted or anonymized.
  • Aggregate network statistics (total supply, total staked, burned totals) — contain no personal data and are unaffected by account deletion.
  • Records we are legally required to keep, retained only as long as the law requires.

Contact privacy@deltixllc.com or support@deltixllc.com. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
